About me

I'm Nandini, a computer science undergraduate at IILM University in Greater Noida, graduating in 2027.

Most of what I build lives on the server: request handling, data models, and the rules that decide who can read what. I also design in Figma, so I usually prototype a screen before I write the controller behind it. That habit has saved me from building endpoints nobody needed.

My favourite problems have a real-time edge. Check-Mate had to decide, while an exam was running, whether a candidate was cheating, and do it fairly. CircleWell had to keep medical data private even though the whole app runs in a browser.

Right now I'm sharpening data structures and algorithms and looking for software engineering roles where I can work on backend systems with a team that reviews code properly.
